The Origin

Our Story

From a passion project in Madhapur to your neighborhood's warm Boho Neon hideaway.

Sabroso

Spanish for 'Tasty'

Beginning

La Sabroso means 'tasty'. We made it a place.

We opened our doors in early 2023 in Madhapur with a simple yet passionate vision: that dining out should nourish more than just appetite. It should celebrate craft, conversation, and memories.

The name gave us our mission. Sabroso — Spanish for 'tasty' — grew into a kitchen where authentic Italian techniques meet the vibrant warmth of Indian spices on the very same plate, prepared with genuine care.

Our room blossomed into what regulars cherish as Boho Neon: macrame accents, warm chandelier glow, comfortable corners, and an ambient energy designed for slow coffee mornings and lively celebrations.
