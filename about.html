The Origin

Our Story

From a passion project in Madhapur to your neighborhood's Boho Neon hideaway.

Sabroso

Spanish for 'Tasty'

Beginning

La Sabroso means 'tasty'. We made it a place.

We opened our doors in early 2023 in Madhapur, the passion project of founder Chengalpet Venu Madhav — a food lover who walked out of the corporate world with a stubborn idea: that a meal should feed more than hunger.

The name gave us the brief. Sabroso — Spanish for 'tasty' — became a room where Italian elegance meets Indian spice on the same table, where a slow braise and a masala both get the respect they deserve.

The room grew into what regulars now call Boho Neon: macrame on the walls, chandeliers hanging low, and a pink neon sign that flickers on every evening. It is warm, a little playful, and deliberately unhurried.

Space & Comfort

Boho Neon, Always.
