About

Where soulful food meets the heartbeat of the neighborhood.

Tucked into Kavuri Hills Road in Madhapur, La Sabroso is a Boho Neon cafe where fairy lights meet marble tables, macrame hangs above vintage mirrors, and every corner is designed for lingering conversations. Our kitchen marries Italian comfort food with Indian soul — pastas, pizzas, artisan coffee, and an ice cream lab that turns dessert into theater.

Bring your laptop, your friends, your family, or your furry companion — we're proudly pet-friendly and open every day from 11 AM to 11 PM.
