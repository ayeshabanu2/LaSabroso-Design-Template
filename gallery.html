Three things our regulars never stop tagging us in.

Ice Cream Lab

Liquid-nitrogen gelato made to order — fresh cream frozen in a rolling cloud of fog, right at your table. The smoothest scoop in Madhapur.

Laso Delights

The chef's seasonal specials board. It changes when the market changes — ask your server what's on it today, because it rarely repeats twice.
